## Stroke Prediction and Insight

**Objective:**  
Enhance stroke risk prediction to enable timely medical intervention and improve patient outcomes. 🧠

---

## What I Achieved
- Developed a machine learning model to accurately forecast stroke risk, achieving **98% accuracy**. 📈  
- Created interactive visualizations to better understand contributing factors and facilitate effective risk management. 📊  
- Designed and implemented Streamlit web applications and Power BI dashboards to enhance user engagement and decision-making. 🌐  

---

## Key Findings
- Individuals aged **57 to 82** have a higher probability of stroke. 🧓  
- **Married** individuals are at a higher risk of stroke. 💍  
- Stroke risk is **not significantly affected** by gender or rural/urban residency. 🚻🏡  
- Adults with **high BMI** levels (indicative of obesity) have an increased stroke risk. ⚖️  
- Older adults tend to have **higher glucose levels**. 🍬  
- The average age for **heart disease** is 71, while for **hypertension** it is 63. 🏥  
- **Regular or former smokers** exhibit higher stroke probability. 🚬  
- Individuals working in government or private sectors show a higher average BMI (~30). 🏢  
- **Private sector** workers have a higher likelihood of stroke and hypertension. 📈  
- **High glucose levels** are associated with higher stroke risk. 🍭
